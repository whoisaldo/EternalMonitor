How it works

Three steps. No drivers to install, no account to create.

1

Run the host

Launch EternalMonitor on your Windows PC. It captures your display with DXGI and encodes with hardware H.264 (NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel).

2

Connect your iPad

Open the iPad app and enter your PC's IP address. Both devices must be on the same WiFi network.

3

Start streaming

Your iPad is now a second display. Frames are streamed over UDP, decoded with VideoToolbox, and rendered with Metal.

Requirements

Windows

  • Windows 10/11 64-bit
  • NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel GPU (NVIDIA recommended for lowest latency)
  • Same WiFi network as iPad

iPad

  • iPadOS 16 or later
  • iPad Pro / Air / mini
  • 120Hz ProMotion supported
